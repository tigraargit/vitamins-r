# VITAMIN R

A self-portrait made of saved references: 43 reels from my Instagram saves, all playing at once in an endless scroll. Tap one to stop everything and look at just that one.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages)
1. New public repository on github.com, e.g. `vitamin-r`.
2. Add file → Upload files → drag in `index.html`, `README.md` and the whole `clips` folder.
3. Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main → / (root) → Save.
4. Live after a minute at `https://<username>.github.io/vitamin-r/`.

## Change the words between the videos
In `index.html`, find `const LINES=[` and write your own lines.

## Presenting
- **auto**: the page scrolls by itself.
- **sound**: turns on the sound of every visible video at once.
- Tap a video: everything else stops. Write why you saved it; it's remembered in that browser.
